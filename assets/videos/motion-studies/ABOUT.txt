60BASE — Original motion studies
Created: 2026-09-10

Six original, procedurally authored motion graphics for the 60BASE homepage.
Each clip is 5.00 seconds, 1280 x 720, 30 fps, silent H.264 MP4.

kitchen: ingredient pinch and release
retail: reach, grasp and place a package
fnb: grasp and lift a cup
logistics: carry a parcel with two hands
care: pass a small object between hands
factory: precision rotation of a component

Hands, objects, 21 landmarks per hand and motion paths are authored geometry.
This is a concept visualization, not recorded egocentric footage, a working
hand-recognition system, model inference, measured accuracy or collected data.
No third-party footage or hand-model assets are incorporated in these clips.

Reproducible source: tools/motion-studies/scene.js and render.mjs in the local
project repository. Source tools and review artifacts are not published.
